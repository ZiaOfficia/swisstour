Good to know

Questions, answered

Which pass is right for me?

As a rule of thumb: three or more travel days in a week usually favours the Swiss Travel Pass. Longer, slower trips with rest days favour the Half Fare Card or the Flex pass. Send us your rough itinerary and we'll compare all three for you.

Do I need a seat reservation?

Not on regular trains — turn up and travel. Panoramic services such as the Glacier Express, Bernina Express and GoldenPass Panoramic do require a reservation, and they sell out in high season, so book early.

How and when do I get my tickets?

Everything is digital. Once payment is confirmed, your tickets and passes arrive by email — usually within seconds — as a PDF with a scannable code. Show it on your phone; no printing or collection needed.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Most passes can be cancelled free of charge up to the day before the first day of validity. Seat reservations on panoramic trains follow the operator's own rules. The exact terms are always shown before you pay.

Do children travel free?

Children under 6 always travel free. Children aged 6–15 travel free when accompanied by a parent holding a Swiss Family Card, which we issue at no cost with your booking.

Are there any booking fees?

None. The price you see is the price you pay — no service charge, no card fee, no currency mark-up at checkout.
